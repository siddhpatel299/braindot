Tutor

And something that has read all of it.

Ask across the whole vault and get answers with the notes they came from. Or hand it a topic and it teaches, asking you questions back and drawing what it explains straight into the note you are studying from.

Your data

The exit is not hidden.

Publish one note, or a folder and everything nested inside it, to a link that needs no account. A published page is a snapshot, so your later edits stay private until you say otherwise, and it is unlisted unless you ask for it to be found.

And whenever you want, the whole vault walks out as a folder of markdown files that open in anything. An export you cannot take when the service is down is not much of an export.
