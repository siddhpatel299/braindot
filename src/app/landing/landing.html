A note is worth what it connects to.

Braindot links what you write to everything else you have written, as you type. You get the backlinks, the graph, a reader that brings sources in, and a tutor that can see all of it at once.

Atomic notes

one idea per note · written for whoever you are tomorrow

A note should hold one idea completely. The power is not in any single note but in what it touches: a note linking to ten others is worth more than ten that sit alone.

This is the claim behind , and the reason works better on small notes than long ones: you can only review what you can hold in one piece.

Which is also why belongs here rather than in its own chapter.

The exit is not hidden.

Publish one note, or a folder and everything nested inside it, to a link that needs no account. A published page is a snapshot, so your later edits stay private until you say otherwise, and it is unlisted unless you ask for it to be found. Links to notes you did not publish quietly stay plain text instead of turning into dead ends.

And whenever you want, the whole vault walks out as a folder of markdown files that open in anything. An export you cannot take when the service is down is not much of an export.
